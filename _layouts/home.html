What I Do

I'm an architect with 19+ years of experience building cloud platforms and distributed systems on AWS, and helping teams modernize the ones that have grown hard to change. These days I focus on solving real business problems with enterprise GenAI and agentic AI, using AI for software delivery (AIFSD) and spec-driven development to ship faster without getting sloppy.

My Approach

I keep things simple: understand the problem first, then choose the design. I stay curious by trying new tools, contributing to open source and writing up what I learn on this blog. And I work best with teams where people share what they know and help each other grow.
