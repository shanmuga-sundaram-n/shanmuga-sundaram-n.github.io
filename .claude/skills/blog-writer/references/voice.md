# Voice and style: Shan's blog

Derived from existing posts (e.g. `2026-10-20-stop-using-llm-for-everything-jev-ai.md`). Re-read one recent post before drafting.

## Voice
- First person, practitioner. "I built", "I profiled", "what I use instead". Hands-on architect with 19+ years; opinions are earned from production, not theory.
- Direct and calm. State the claim, show the evidence, name the trade-off. Skeptical of hype, including about tools Shan likes.
- Plain words. Short sentences mixed with the occasional longer one. Concrete nouns, real numbers, named tools and versions.
- Always include where the approach does NOT fit.

## Structure habits
- Opening: the situation and the doubt or problem, in 2-3 short paragraphs.
- Body: H2 per idea, each opening with its conclusion; diagrams or code to show the mechanism; a cost/latency/risk comparison when relevant.
- Close: a "Decision Rule"-style section: when to use it, when not to.

## Avoid (AI-slop tells)
- Openers: "In today's...", "In the ever-evolving...", "Let's dive in", "Have you ever wondered".
- Filler: "game-changer", "revolutionary", "unlock", "leverage", "seamlessly", "robust", "delve", "landscape", "tapestry".
- Rhetorical-contrast tics: "It's not X, it's Y" repeated; triplet lists in every paragraph; heavy em-dash use.
- Closers: "In conclusion", "To sum up", "Happy coding".
- Unsourced superlatives and invented stats.
- Emoji in headings or body.

## Length guide
short ~1000 | standard 1800-2500 | deep 3500-4500 words (the SDLC and harness posts are deep dives).
